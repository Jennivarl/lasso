Two parts, one lasso

Lending apps only handle price drops. When a real business stops paying, nobody decides who’s right or who gets paid first, and nobody can reach collateral on another chain. Portaldot calls the fix its flagship: Default-as-a-Service.

PORTALDOT

The court

Runs the loan with a senior and a junior lender. If the borrower stops paying, a lender files a claim with a bond, the borrower can answer, 2 of 3 arbiters decide, and it issues a ruling.

⇄proof of lock →
← the ruling
ETHEREUM

The long arm

A vault holds the borrower’s ETH. It pays out only on a valid ruling: senior first, then junior, then the surplus back to the borrower. Exactly once.
