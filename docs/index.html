The ruling happens on Portaldot

Each loan has a senior and a junior lender. After the due date and a grace period, a lender can file a claim with a bond. The borrower can answer with evidence, and two of three arbiters decide. No answer, or no decision in time, and the default stands.

The collateral stays on Ethereum

LassoVault holds the ETH for the life of the loan. It moves only when a ruling from Portaldot arrives, pays exactly what the ruling lists, and pays once. Today one signer carries messages between the chains; the next step replaces it with zero-knowledge proofs.
