Portaldot

The court

Loans with a senior and a junior lender. Miss the due date and a lender files a claim with a bond. The borrower can answer with evidence; 2 of 3 arbiters decide. The ruling says who gets the collateral: senior, then junior, then any surplus back to the borrower.

Ethereum

The long arm

The ETH stays in LassoVault on Ethereum the whole time. It moves only when a valid Portaldot ruling arrives, it pays exactly what the ruling says, and it pays once. Today a signer carries the messages; next, zero-knowledge proofs.
